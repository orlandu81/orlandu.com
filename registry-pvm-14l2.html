About this model

Catalog notes: 600 TVL Trinitron; 15 kHz; RGB/YPbPr; 1 option slot. Era: c.2001–2006, manual copyright or brochure print year (Sony US brochure printed 12/02; Sony Japan lineup page; collector consensus early 2000s). Sony never published production figures for any of these; the registry is the count.
