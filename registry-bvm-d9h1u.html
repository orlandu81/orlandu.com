About this model

Catalog notes: 450 TVL HR Trinitron P-22, 70 deg; multiformat 15–45 kHz; display unit (no audio); battery option. Era: 1999–2005, Sony press release, bulletin or brochure date (Sony Japan press 2000-03-09: D series released Oct 1999 (J models); US brochure printed 4/00; Sony bulletin 05-0584: D series discontinued 2005-12-31; Sony bulletin Jan 2005: 'BVM-D9 series' replaced by LMD-9050). Sony never published production figures for any of these; the registry is the count.
