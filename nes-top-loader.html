Console Profile

NES — Top Loader

Nintendo’s 1993 redesign of the NES. Out of the box it only spoke RF; this one puts out RGB.

MakerNintendo
ModelNES-101
Year1993
VideoRGB (modded)

Nintendo sold the top loader at the very end of the NES’s run: a smaller, cheaper box with the cartridge going straight down, the way the Famicom always had it. It dropped the front loader’s spring-loaded tray and the lockout chip along with it, so the blinking-screen ritual of the original console never happens here.

It also dropped the front loader’s AV jacks. Stock, the top loader speaks RF only, the weakest picture the NES ever had.

The NESRGB board fixes that at the source. It takes the color straight from the console’s picture chip, so this NES joins the rest of the shelf in RGB: an RGB SCART cable into the switcher, a SCART to 4 BNC breakout out of it, and a Sony monitor at the end.

The console mod table lists every machine’s path to the monitors, and The Signal Chain walks it box by box. The NES Front Loader carries an NESRGB board too; it isn’t on the display shelf. Nintendo’s own Player’s Guides have a shelf of their own, and the arcade side of the NES library is on the PlayChoice-10 wall.
