What Sony's bulletins say

Sony's technical bulletins list the serial numbers each firmware update covers. The highest number listed for a badge is a ceiling: how far that serial run had reached by the bulletin's date, not a production total. European (E) and U.S. (U) sets are numbered separately; the 1998 bulletin, written for the Americas, shows the U sets starting at 2,000,001 before moving to the 2,100,000 block. The figures are copied exactly as Sony printed them, including the few that don't agree from one bulletin to the next.

About this model

Catalog notes: 900 TVL 0.30 mm HR Trinitron (same CRT family as PVM-20L5/20M4); 1x RGB/YPbPr built in; BKM-2xx cards; needs BKM-10R/11R. Era: c.1996–2001, manual copyright or brochure print year (consolemods infobox: earliest Mar 1996 (no Sony date cited); Sony TB 2001-230 (Jul 2001) lists all E/F/G in service; superseded by D series Oct 1999 and G series 1998; ex-Sony tech: most widely produced 20in BVM; Sony TB 1999-274R (Dec 2000) and 2001-236 (Jul 2001) list E and U serial ceilings still rising into mid-2001). Sony never published production totals; the bulletin ceilings above are the nearest thing, and the registry is the count.
