Console Profile

Nintendo 64

Nintendo’s 1996 cartridge holdout. The American N64 never put RGB on its back; this one does.

MakerNintendo
ModelNUS-001
Year1996
VideoRGB (modded)

The Nintendo 64 reached North America in September 1996, the last major home console built around cartridges. Four controller ports on the front made it the multiplayer machine of its generation.

Its video is the catch. The American console sends composite and S-Video out of its multi-out and nothing else; RGB isn’t on the connector at all.

The RGB upgrade puts it there, so the N64 takes the same road to the monitors as the rest of the shelf: an RGB SCART cable into the switcher, a SCART to 4 BNC breakout out of it, and a Sony monitor at the end.
