Foreverspin, Metal by Metal

The Spinning Top Wall

Foreverspin makes one spinning top, the same shape every time, and turns it in a long list of metals. Line them up and the only thing left to compare is the material: its color, its weight in the hand, the way it wears. Each top here is photographed off its base and labeled with the metal it is made of.

Mystery tops and limited editions

Foreverspin slips mystery tops into orders as gifts and releases limited editions in colors that come and go. The company doesn’t say what the mystery tops are made of, so these are labeled by color and edition instead.
