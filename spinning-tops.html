Quick answers

Which spinning top is the heaviest, and which the lightest?

Tungsten, at 60 g — Foreverspin calls it the master top — and magnesium at 6 g, ten times lighter and the fastest-spinning of them all. The same shape, so the difference is entirely the metal.

How much does a tungsten spinning top weigh?

Foreverspin’s tungsten top is 60 g. For scale, the stainless steel original is 28 g, titanium 16 g, silver 37 g and copper 32 g; every top on this page is labeled with its weight.

What steel spinning tops are there?

Stainless Steel (the 28 g original), S.S. Mirror with its 10-day polish, Stainless Steel Damascus, M2 high-speed steel, Golden-Inox and CarbonChromium — six steels, each a different pattern or finish on the same form.

Are all Foreverspin tops the same shape?

Yes. One shape, turned in a long list of metals, which is the point of lining them up: the only thing left to compare is the material — its color, its weight in the hand and the way it wears.

What are Foreverspin mystery tops?

Tops the company slips into orders as gifts, alongside limited editions in colors that come and go. Foreverspin doesn’t say what they are made of, so the twenty-five here are labeled by color and edition, all 9 g.
