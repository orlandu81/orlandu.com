The Orlandu 100

About 24 min read

One collector's hundred favorite films, ordered by head-to-head duel rather than by argument — every position on this list was won against another film. The scores were pulled from Rotten Tomatoes. Nobody consulted a critic about the order.

Critics83
Audience85

Colophon How the order was made, and what to distrust

How the order was made. 418 films from a personal library export and 162 more from a second ballot were cut to 240 keepers, sorted into bands, then ranked inside each band by direct head-to-head comparison — merge sort, one duel at a time. A final pass of 18 duels repaired the joints between bands, where the ranking was weakest. Everything below the hundredth place was cut.

What to distrust. The sort never re-tests a comparison, so one fast call can carry a film several places. Treat a gap of two or three positions as noise and a gap of twenty as real. The top eight are the most tested and the most trustworthy.

Sources and dates. Critic and audience scores, MPAA ratings and runtimes come from Rotten Tomatoes, fetched September 4, 2026 — audience scores drift, so these are a snapshot. Directors, casts, budgets and worldwide grosses come from Wikipedia and Box Office Mojo. 17 budgets and 5 gross figures are omitted where sources give only a range or no figure at all; nothing here is estimated. Grosses include later re-releases.

The ranking is Orlandu's. The synopses are editorial and deliberately avoid spoiling the films that turn on a reveal.

This one is off the usual beat — no CRTs, no coin doors. It is here because a hundred films ranked by duel is the same instinct as a shelf of monitors ranked by measurement: the argument is settled by comparison, not by opinion.
