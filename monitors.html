The fleet, to scale

Every model drawn to Sony’s published width and height on one floor line, with an NES for size. The side view is the part nobody puts in words: a 20-inch BVM is deeper than it is wide, and the panel that replaced it is seven centimeters thick.

Front and side elevations from the dimensions Sony prints in each model’s brochure or operation manual, without projecting parts. The BVM-A14F5U is drawn as its bezel inside the dashed 19-inch rack frame the U model ships in. The LMD-9020 and 9030 are shown on their supplied stands; the 9050 is one piece.
