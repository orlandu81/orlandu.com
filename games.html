In memoriam machines that passed through on their way to someone else's story

Console library

Beyond the cabinets there's a cataloged collection of consoles and games — nearly 900 titles logged, from the NES shelf to the TurboGrafx HuCards. The hardware has its own page, and the PlayChoice-10 game boards that ran in cabinets like the one in the memoriam above have a wall of their own — all fifty-two Nintendo released. And if a word on these pages — JAMMA, CPO, shop-out, candy cab — went by without an explanation, the glossary has it. Every machine here, and every console and monitor, is also on one timeline, in the order each was released.
